# Nigerian Education Standards & Examination Systems

> Reference document for Academio's education domain modeling.
> Last researched: July 2025

---

## 1. Education Structure (1-6-3-3-4)

Nigeria follows the **National Policy on Education (2013 edition)** with a 1-6-3-3-4 structure:

| Level | Duration | Ages | Institution | Certification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early Childhood (Crèche) | 1 year | 0–2 | Crèche / Daycare | None |
| Pre-Primary (Nursery) | 2 years | 3–4 | Nursery School | None (internal) |
| Primary | 6 years | 5–10 | Primary School | **Primary School Leaving Certificate (PSLC)** |
| Junior Secondary (JSS) | 3 years | 11–13 | Junior Secondary School | **BECE** (Basic Education Certificate Examination) |
| Senior Secondary (SSS) | 3 years | 14–16 | Senior Secondary School | **WASSCE / WAEC / NECO** |
| Tertiary (University) | 4 years (min) | 17+ | University / Polytechnic / College of Education | **Bachelor's Degree / ND / NCE** |

**Key policy points:**
- 9 years of **compulsory basic education** (6 primary + 3 junior secondary)
- Free Universal Basic Education (UBE) programme covers primary + JSS
- Transition from primary to JSS is automatic (no entrance exam)
- Transition from JSS to SSS requires **BECE**
- JSS and SSS have a **3-year** duration each (not 2 as in some countries)

## 2. Primary School (Primary 1–6)

### Core Subjects (All Classes)

| Subject | Hours/Week (typical) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 5–6 | Compulsory all 6 years |
| English Language | 5–6 | Compulsory; introduced from P1 |
| Basic Science & Technology | 3–4 | Integrated science, pre-vocational studies, IT |
| Social Studies | 2–3 | Civics, culture, history |
| Cultural & Creative Arts | 2 | Art, music, drama |
| Nigerian Languages | 3–4 | At least one major language (Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba) |
| Physical & Health Education | 2 | Sports, hygiene, first aid |
| Religious Studies | 2 | Christian Religious Studies (CRS) or Islamic Studies (IRS) — varies by region |
| Agricultural Science | 2 | From P4 onward (vocational prep) |
| Home Economics | 1–2 | Mainly P4–P6 |
| Civic Education | 1–2 | National values, human rights |

### Regional Language Variations
- **North (Hausa-majority):** Hausa as primary language, English introduced gradually
- **South-West (Yoruba-majority):** Yoruba as primary language
- **South-East (Igbo-majority):** Igbo as primary language
- **South-South:** Various minority languages + English dominance
- National Policy mandates **mother-tongue instruction** in early primary, transitioning to English

### Assessment (Primary)
- **Continuous Assessment (CA):** 30–40% of final grade
- **End-of-term exams:** 30–40%
- **Final year exams (P6):** 20–30% — determines placement for BECE
- No national exam at primary level; PSLC is school-issued
- **Federal Government** conducts no primary-level national exam (unlike WAEC at SS level)
- Some states administer their own **common entrance exams** for selective secondary placement

| Basic Science | 3 | Integrated science |

| Civic Education | 2 | Democracy, human rights |
| Agricultural Science | 2 | Farming, food processing |
| Home Economics | 2 | Domestic skills |
| Physical & Health Education | 2 | Sports, health |
| Christian Religious Studies (CRS) | 2 | Elective (varies) |
| Islamic Studies | 2 | Elective (varies) |
| Nigerian Languages | 3 | At least one (Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba) |
| French | 2 | Optional; offered in many schools |
| Business Studies | 2 | From JSS2 (vocational prep) |
| Computer Studies / ICT | 2 | From JSS1 (practical IT skills) |

### Elective/Technical Subjects (from JSS2)
- Technical Drawing
- Visual Arts
- Music
- Home Economics (deeper)
- Basic Electronics
- Crop Production
- Animal Husbandry

### BECE (Basic Education Certificate Examination)
- **Conducted by:** WAEC (on behalf of states) — "Junior WAEC"
- **Eligibility:** JSS3 students
- **Exam body:** WAEC administers, but **state education ministries** organize registration
- **Subjects examined:** Typically **9–11 subjects**
  - 6 compulsory: Mathematics, English, Social Studies, Civic Education, Basic Science, Basic Technology
  - 3–5 electives: Nigerian Language, CRS/IRS, Agricultural Science, Home Economics, French, Computer Studies
- **Grading:**

  | Grade | Score Range | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|
  | A | 80–100 | Excellent |
  | B | 60–79 | Very Good |
  | C | 50–59 | Good |
  | P | 40–49 | Pass |
  | F | 0–39 | Fail |

- **Pass requirement:** Minimum **6 subjects** including **English and Mathematics** at grade C or above
- **Result validity:** Used for placement into SSS; does not affect university admission

## 5. Examination Systems

### 5.1 WAEC (West African Examinations Council)

**Established:** 1952
**Members:** Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, Liberia, The Gambia
**Headquarters:** Accra, Ghana (Nigeria office: Lagos)

#### WASSCE (West African Senior School Certificate Examination)

**Internal (May/June):** For school candidates (SS3 students registered through schools)
- **Eligibility:** SS3 students only; must be registered by their school
- **Exam period:** May–June annually
- **Results:** Released ~60–90 days after exams
- **Grading:**

  | Grade | Score Range | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|
  | A1 | 75–100 | Excellent |
  | B2 | 70–74 | Very Good |
  | B3 | 65–69 | Good (Credit) |
  | C4 | 60–64 | Credit |
  | C5 | 55–59 | Credit |
  | C6 | 50–59 | Credit |
  | D7 | 45–49 | Pass |
  | E8 | 40–44 | Pass |
  | F9 | 0–39 | Fail |

- **Minimum for university admission:** **5 credits** (C6 or above) including **English Language** and **Mathematics**
- **Some competitive programs** require credits in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, etc.
- **Duration validity:** Results valid for **unlimited** sittings (some institutions prefer recent)

**External (Nov/Dec):** For private candidates
- **Eligibility:** Anyone (private candidates not in school)
- **Exam period:** November–December annually
- **Same grading and credit requirements as Internal**
- **Results:** Released ~60–90 days after exams

### 5.2 NECO (National Examinations Council)

**Established:** 1977 (decree); exams started 1982
**Role:** Federal Government alternative to WAEC

#### NECO SSCE (Senior School Certificate Examination)

**Internal (June/July):** For school candidates
- **Eligibility:** SS3 students registered through schools
- **Exam period:** June–July annually
- **Grading:** Same as WAEC (A1–F9 scale)
- **Credits** treated equivalently to WAEC by universities
- **2026 updates:**
  - **Dual mode:** CBE (Computer-Based Examination) + PPE (Paper-Pencil Examination)
  - **NIN (National Identification Number) compulsory** for registration
  - **NLIN** assigned after NIN verification
  - **SS3-only eligibility** — SS2 students no longer allowed to sit

**External (Oct/Nov):** For private candidates
- Same structure as WAEC External
- Grading equivalent

#### NECO BECE
- **Basic Education Certificate Examination** for JSS3 students
- Conducted by NECO as alternative to WAEC Junior WAEC
- Same grading scale as WAEC BECE

### 5.3 JAMB/UTME (Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board)

**Established:** 1978
**Role:** Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination — the **gateway to university**

#### UTME (Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination)
- **Eligibility:** SSCE holders (WAEC/NECO credits) and awaiting-result candidates
- **Format:** Computer-Based Test (CBT)
- **Subjects:** 4 subjects including **Use of English** (compulsory)
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Subject combinations by course:**

  | Course | Subject Combination |
  |---|---|
  | Medicine | Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics |
  | Engineering | Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry |
  | Law | Use of English, Literature in English, Government/Civic Ed, CRS/IRS |
  | Business Admin | Use of English, Mathematics, Economics, Government |
  | Computer Science | Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry/Biology |
  | Education | Use of English, two subjects from chosen teaching area |
  | Agriculture | Use of English, Biology/Agricultural Science, Chemistry, Physics/Maths |
  | Mass Communication | Use of English, Literature in English, Government, CRS/IRS |

- **Marking:** 100 marks total; **40% for Use of English**, 20% per other subject
- **Minimum for university:** Varies by institution (typically 180–200 out of 400)
- **Catchment area/indigenization:** Lower cut-offs for catchment candidates
- **Direct Entry (DE):** For ND/HND holders bypassing UTME (apply directly to 200-level)

### 5.4 Common Entrance Examination
- **National Common Entrance Examination (NCEE):** For admission into Federal Unity Schools (104 unity schools nationwide)
- **Eligibility:** P6 students (Primary 6)
- **Subjects:** English, Mathematics, General Paper
- **Conducted by:** National Examinations Council (NECO)
- **Purpose:** Selective admission to federal government colleges
- **State-level common entrance:** Many states run their own for secondary placement

## 6. Compliance & Regulatory Requirements

### 6.1 Governing Bodies

| Body | Role | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Ministry of Education (FME)** | Policy, standards, oversight | National |
| **National Universities Commission (NUC)** | University accreditation, curriculum | Tertiary |
| **National Board for Technical Education (NBTE)** | Polytechnic/monotech accreditation | Tertiary |
| **National Commission for Colleges of Education (NCCE)** | CoE accreditation | Tertiary |
| **States Universal Basic Education Board (SUBEB)** | Basic education implementation | State-level |
| **West African Examinations Council (WAEC)** | Exam administration (WASSCE, BECE) | Regional (5 countries) |
| **National Examinations Council (NECO)** | Exam administration (SSCE, BECE) | National |
| **Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB)** | University admission coordination | National |

### 6.2 Key Compliance Requirements for Schools

#### Curriculum Compliance
- Schools **must follow the National Curriculum** as prescribed by FME
- **NERDC (Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council)** develops and reviews curricula
- Minimum subject offerings mandated at each level
- Schools may add **additional subjects** beyond the minimum
- **Language policy:** Mother-tongue instruction in early primary, transitioning to English

#### Registration & Accreditation
- Schools must be **registered with the state Ministry of Education**
- **Federal Government Colleges** (Unity Schools) registered with FME
- WAEC/NECO school registration: schools must apply and be approved to register candidates
- **NECO 2026 requirement:** Schools must verify students' NIN before registration

#### Examination Compliance
- Schools **must register all eligible SS3 students** for at least one of WAEC or NECO
- **Double registration** (both WAEC and NECO) is permitted and common
- Schools must maintain **exam centers** meeting WAEC/NECO standards (space, seating, security)
- **Malpractice prevention:** Schools must have internal committees; penalties include school closure

#### Teacher Qualification
- All teachers must hold **minimum NCE (National Certificate in Education)** or equivalent
- University teachers require **PhD or Master's + teaching experience**
- **Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN)** handles teacher certification
- Schools must maintain teacher-to-student ratios (varies by level)

#### Financial & Reporting
- Schools must submit **annual returns** to state education boards
- **UBE schools** receive government funding and must comply with UBE reporting
- Fee structures for government schools are regulated by state governments
- Private schools set own fees but must comply with state regulations

### 6.3 Data & Record-Keeping Requirements
- Schools must maintain **student records** for minimum 7 years
- **Result sheets** must be submitted to WAEC/NECO after exams
- **Attendance records** mandatory for exam eligibility
- **Continuous Assessment records** must be available for inspection
- NECO 2026: NIN verification data must be retained for audit trail

## 7. Grading Systems Summary

### WAEC/NECO WASSCE (SSS Level)

| Grade | Score | Credit? | University Eligible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 75–100 | Yes | Yes |
| B2 | 70–74 | Yes | Yes |
| B3 | 65–69 | Yes | Yes |
| C4 | 60–64 | Yes | Yes |
| C5 | 55–59 | Yes | Yes |
| C6 | 50–59 | Yes | Yes |
| D7 | 45–49 | Pass | No (not a credit) |
| E8 | 40–44 | Pass | No (not a credit) |
| F9 | 0–39 | Fail | No |

**University requirement:** 5 credits including English + Mathematics

### BECE (Junior Secondary Level)

| Grade | Score Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | 80–100 | Excellent |
| B | 60–79 | Very Good |
| C | 50–59 | Good |
| P | 40–49 | Pass |
| F | 0–39 | Fail |

### JAMB/UTME
- **Total:** 400 marks (100 per subject)
- **Use of English:** 60 questions (40 marks, 40%)
- **Other subjects:** 40 questions each (20 marks each, 20%)
- **Minimum score for university:** 180–200 (varies by institution)
- **Score validity:** One academic session only

## 8. Academio Domain Modeling Implications

### Recommended Entity Structure

```
School
  -> Level (Nursery, Primary, JSS, SSS)
    -> Class (P1-P6, JSS1-JSS3, SSS1-SSS3)
      -> Subject (based on level + track)
        -> Assessment (continuous, term, exam)
          -> Score / Grade
            -> Report Card

Student
  -> Enrollment (School, Class, Session)
  -> Track (Science, Humanities, Commercial) — SSS only
  -> NIN (for NECO/JAMB registration)

Examination
  -> Type (BECE, WASSCE-Internal, WASSCE-External, NECO-SSCE, UTME)
  -> Session (year)
  -> Subjects registered
  -> Results / Grades
```

### Key Grade Calculation Rules
1. **Primary/JSS:** Term aggregate = weighted average (CA + Exams)
2. **SSS:** WAEC/NECO standard grading (A1-F9)
3. **BECE:** Pass/Fail for transition (not used for university)
4. **JAMB:** Separate scoring system (400 total)
5. **Position ranking:** Class position based on aggregate score

### Platform Must Support
- Multiple grading scales (primary vs JSS vs SSS)
- Track-based subject assignment (SSS)
- Dual exam registration (WAEC + NECO)
- NIN integration for NECO/JAMB
- Continuous Assessment tracking with configurable weights
- Report cards with per-subject grades + aggregates
- BECE result generation for JSS3 transitions
- JAMB subject combination validation
